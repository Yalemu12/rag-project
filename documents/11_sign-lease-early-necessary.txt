[score 1] Sign now if you want a particular unit in a specific building. If you’re flexible, you have more time. Rates increase as months go by. Leftover units in the summer might have promos of lower parking rates or even reduced rent just to fill spots. 

Resigning leases in your current unit guarantee you won’t have to deal with moving, can lock in your rate, and you won’t have that homeless two-week gap in the summer. 

There are pros and cons to both signing and waiting. Decide what’s most important to you.
